What is Critical Role?

Critical Role is an American web series in which a group of professional voice actors play Dungeons & Dragons. The show started streaming partway through the cast's first campaign in March 2015.

Campaign one ended in October 2017 after 115 episodes, and campaign two started in January 2018 and ended in June 2021 after 141 episodes. A number of one-shots were aired in the hiatus between the two campaigns. After campaign two was completed, the spin-off limited series Exandria Unlimited aired from June 2021 to August 2021. The third campaign aired from October 21, 2021 to February 6, 2025 with 121 episodes. The fourth campaign premiered on October 2, 2025.

The first three campaigns featured Matthew Mercer as the show's Dungeon Master and were set in the Exandria campaign setting; Brennan Lee Mulligan of Dimension 20 is the Game Master for the fourth campaign which is set in Mulligan's setting of Aramán.

You can visit the website for Critical Role for more information.

What is Daggerheart?

Daggerheart is a fantasy tabletop roleplaying game where players create characters and tell a shared story together. Its Hope and Fear dice help shape the outcome—and the direction—of each dramatic moment.

You can visit the Daggerheart website for more information.

What is Hololive?

Hololive Production (ホロライブプロダクション), or simply known as hololive (ホロライブ), is a Virtual Talent agency consisting of Virtual YouTubers owned by Japanese tech entertainment company COVER Corporation.

It consists of 66 talents, including 38 Japanese, 11 Indonesian, and 17 English-speaking Virtual YouTubers. The agency also has a branch called Holostars, which consists of male Virtual YouTubers.

You can visit the Hololive website for more information.

Locations

The Shattered Teeth

The Shattered Teeth is a major archipelago of 43 islands, located on Exandria southeast of Tal'Dorei and far south of Wildemount, past the persistent fogbank known as the Fool's Curtain. Varying in size from mile-wide reef-top isles to large, city-holding islands like Ruukva, this archipelago is mainly split between two conflicting societies: the Ossended Host and the Wanderman Assembly.
